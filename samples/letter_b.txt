Dear Policyholder,

We refer to your claim for hospitalisation expenses amounting to Rs. 92,500 (Rupees Ninety-Two Thousand Five Hundred only) in respect of your admission at Lakeview Hospital on 02 August 2026 for treatment of a hypertensive crisis.

On review of your claim, it has come to our notice that you suffer from hypertension, which was not disclosed by you in the proposal form submitted at the time of purchasing this policy (policy inception date: 10 January 2025). As per Section 6 (Disclosure and Non-Disclosure) of your policy wording, non-disclosure of a material fact known to the insured person prior to the effective date of the policy entitles the insurer to repudiate the claim.

We regret to inform you that your claim stands rejected on account of non-disclosure of a pre-existing condition.

You may write to our Grievance Redressal Officer if you wish to contest this decision.
