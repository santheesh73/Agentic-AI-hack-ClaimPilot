Dear Policyholder,

We refer to your claim for Rs. 62,000 for cataract surgery (right eye) at Vision Eye Hospital on 03 October 2026.

As per Clause 4.3 (Specified Disease/Procedure Waiting Period) and Excl03 of your policy wording, expenses for treatment of cataract are excluded until the expiry of 24 months of continuous coverage. Cataract surgery is a listed procedure under this clause.

Your claim therefore stands rejected.

You may write to our Grievance Redressal Officer if you wish to contest this decision.
