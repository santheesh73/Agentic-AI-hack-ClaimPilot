Dear Policyholder,

We refer to your claim for Rs. 88,000 for your admission at Ruby Hall Clinic on 02 October 2026 for treatment of a complication of hypertension. Hypertension was declared by you as a pre-existing disease in the proposal form. Your policy with us commenced on 01 December 2024.

As per Clause 4.1 (Pre-existing Diseases) of your policy wording, expenses related to a pre-existing disease are excluded until the expiry of 48 months of continuous coverage. You have completed 22 months of coverage with us.

Your claim therefore stands rejected.

You may write to our Grievance Redressal Officer if you wish to contest this decision.
