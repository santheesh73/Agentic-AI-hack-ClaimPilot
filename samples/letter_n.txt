Dear Policyholder,

We refer to your claim for Rs. 2,40,000 for your admission at Manipal Hospital on 12 October 2026 for treatment of chronic kidney disease. Your policy commenced on 15 January 2025.

As per Clause 6.2 of your policy wording, the insurer may repudiate a claim where a condition was not disclosed in the proposal form. Kidney disease was not mentioned in your proposal form.

Your claim therefore stands rejected on grounds of non-disclosure.

You may write to our Grievance Redressal Officer if you wish to contest this decision.
