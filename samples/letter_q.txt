Dear Policyholder,

We refer to your claim for Rs. 1,05,000 for your admission at Lifeline Hospital on 16 October 2026 for treatment of a fracture of the left tibia and fibula. Your first health insurance policy with us commenced on 01 October 2026.

As per Clause 4.2 (First 30-day Waiting Period) and Excl02 of your policy wording, expenses related to the treatment of any illness within 30 days from the first policy commencement date are excluded. The admission took place 15 days after commencement.

Your claim therefore stands rejected.

You may write to our Grievance Redressal Officer if you wish to contest this decision.
