Subject: Rejection of Hospitalisation Claim

Dear Policyholder,

We refer to your claim for Rs. 58,000 for your admission at Sahyadri Hospital on 07 October 2026 for an acute asthma attack. Your policy commenced on 01 June 2025.

Our investigation found pharmacy records and a pulmonologist's prescription dated 14 March 2023 showing that you were diagnosed with bronchial asthma and prescribed inhalers more than two years before the policy started. The proposal form you signed answered 'No' to the question on respiratory conditions. As per Section 6 (Disclosure and Non-Disclosure), Clause 6.2, of your policy wording, the insurer may repudiate a claim where a condition known to the insured person before the effective date of the policy was not disclosed.

Your claim therefore stands rejected on grounds of non-disclosure of a material fact.

You may write to our Grievance Redressal Officer if you wish to contest this decision.
