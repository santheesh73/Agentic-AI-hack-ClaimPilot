Subject: Rejection of Hospitalisation Claim

Dear Policyholder,

We refer to your claim for Rs. 46,000 for your admission at Sunrise Hospital on 28 September 2026 for treatment of dengue fever. Your policy commenced on 10 September 2026 and you have no prior health insurance policy with any insurer.

As per Clause 4.2 (First 30-day Waiting Period) and Excl02 of your policy wording, expenses related to the treatment of any illness within 30 days from the first policy commencement date are excluded, except claims arising due to an accident. Dengue fever is an illness and not an accident, and the admission occurred 18 days after commencement.

Your claim therefore stands rejected.

You may write to our Grievance Redressal Officer if you wish to contest this decision.
