Dear Policyholder,

We refer to your claim for Rs. 1,35,000 for your admission at Fortis Hospital on 06 October 2026 for coronary angioplasty. Coronary artery disease was declared by you as a pre-existing disease in the proposal form. Your policy with us commenced on 01 July 2025.

As per Excl01 (Pre-Existing Diseases) of your policy wording, expenses related to a pre-existing disease are excluded until the expiry of 48 months of continuous coverage after the date of inception of the first policy with the insurer. You have completed 15 months of coverage with us.

Your claim therefore stands rejected.

You may write to our Grievance Redressal Officer if you wish to contest this decision.
