Subject: Rejection of Hospitalisation Claim

Dear Policyholder,

We refer to your claim for Rs. 96,000 for your admission at Himalaya Hospital on 20 September 2026 for treatment of an ankle fracture sustained while trekking during a family holiday.

As per Excl09 (Hazardous or Adventure Sports) of your policy wording, expenses related to treatment necessitated by participation in hazardous or adventure sports, including mountaineering and rock climbing, are excluded. Trekking in mountainous terrain is treated by us as an adventure sport.

Your claim therefore stands rejected.

You may write to our Grievance Redressal Officer if you wish to contest this decision.
