Dear Policyholder,

We refer to your claim for Rs. 1,48,000 for your admission at City Care Hospital on 22 September 2026 for treatment of a fractured femur sustained in a road traffic accident on the same day. Your policy commenced on 10 September 2026; this is your first health insurance policy.

As per Clause 4.2 (First 30-day Waiting Period) and Excl02 of your policy wording, expenses for treatment within 30 days of the first policy commencement date are excluded. Since the admission occurred 12 days after commencement, the claim falls within the 30-day waiting period.

Your claim therefore stands rejected.

You may write to our Grievance Redressal Officer if you wish to contest this decision.
