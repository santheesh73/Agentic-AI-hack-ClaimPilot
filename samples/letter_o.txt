Dear Policyholder,

We refer to your claim for Rs. 3,20,000 for total hip replacement surgery at Aster Hospital on 09 October 2026. Your first policy with us commenced on 01 September 2025 and you have no prior policy with another insurer.

As per Clause 4.3 of your policy wording, expenses for joint replacement surgery are excluded until the expiry of 24 months of continuous coverage. On the date of surgery you had completed 13 months of continuous coverage.

Your claim therefore stands rejected.

You may write to our Grievance Redressal Officer if you wish to contest this decision.
