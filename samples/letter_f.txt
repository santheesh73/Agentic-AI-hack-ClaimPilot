Dear Policyholder,

We refer to your claim for Rs. 85,000 for inguinal hernia repair surgery at Metro Hospital on 01 October 2026. Your first policy with us commenced on 01 August 2025 and has been renewed without a break; you have no prior policy with another insurer.

As per Clause 4.3 (Specified Disease/Procedure Waiting Period) and Excl03 of your policy wording, expenses for treatment of hernia of all types are excluded until the expiry of 24 months of continuous coverage. On the date of surgery you had completed 14 months of continuous coverage.

Your claim therefore stands rejected.

You may write to our Grievance Redressal Officer if you wish to contest this decision.
