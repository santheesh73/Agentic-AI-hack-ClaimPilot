Dear Policyholder,

We refer to your claim for Rs. 74,000 for your admission at Apollo Clinic Hospital on 30 September 2026 for treatment of a thyroid disorder. Hypothyroidism was declared by you as a pre-existing disease in the proposal form. Your first health insurance policy, with us, commenced on 01 February 2025 and you have no prior policy with any insurer.

As per Clause 4.1 (Pre-existing Diseases) and Excl01 of your policy wording, expenses related to a pre-existing disease are excluded until the expiry of 48 months of continuous coverage. You have completed 20 months of coverage.

Your claim therefore stands rejected.

You may write to our Grievance Redressal Officer if you wish to contest this decision.
