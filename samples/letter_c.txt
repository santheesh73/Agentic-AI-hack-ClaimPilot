Dear Policyholder,

We refer to your claim for hospitalisation expenses amounting to Rs. 1,10,000 (Rupees One Lakh Ten Thousand only) in respect of your admission at CityCare Hospital on 20 July 2026 for rhinoplasty (cosmetic nose reshaping surgery).

On review of your claim and the discharge summary, it is noted that the procedure undergone was a cosmetic/aesthetic surgery and was not necessitated by any accident, burn injury, or cancer treatment. As per Clause Excl08 (Cosmetic or Plastic Surgery) of your policy wording, expenses for cosmetic or plastic surgery undertaken for aesthetic purposes are excluded in all circumstances under the policy.

We regret to inform you that your claim stands rejected on account of the above cosmetic surgery exclusion.

You may write to our Grievance Redressal Officer if you wish to contest this decision.
