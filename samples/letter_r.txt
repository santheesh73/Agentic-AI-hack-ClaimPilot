Dear Policyholder,

We refer to your claim for Rs. 54,000 for your admission at Greenfield Hospital on 18 October 2026 for treatment of viral pneumonia. Your policy with us commenced on 02 October 2026.

As per Clause 4.2 (First 30-day Waiting Period) and Excl02 of your policy wording, expenses related to the treatment of any illness within 30 days from the first policy commencement date are excluded. The admission took place 16 days after commencement.

Your claim therefore stands rejected.

You may write to our Grievance Redressal Officer if you wish to contest this decision.
