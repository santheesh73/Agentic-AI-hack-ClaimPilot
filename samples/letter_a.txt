Dear Policyholder,

We refer to your claim for hospitalisation expenses amounting to Rs. 1,85,000 (Rupees One Lakh Eighty-Five Thousand only) in respect of your admission at Sunrise Multispeciality Hospital on 15 September 2026 for treatment of Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus with diabetic foot ulcer.

On review of your claim and medical records, it is noted that you have a history of Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus diagnosed in the year 2020, prior to the inception of your policy with us (policy inception date: 01 March 2022). Your policy has been continuously renewed without any break since that date, and your sum insured has remained unchanged at Rs. 5,00,000 throughout. As per Clause Excl01 (Pre-Existing Diseases) of your policy wording, expenses related to the treatment of a pre-existing disease and its direct complications are excluded.

We regret to inform you that your claim stands rejected on account of the above pre-existing disease exclusion.

You may write to our Grievance Redressal Officer if you wish to contest this decision.
