Dear Policyholder,

We refer to your claim for Rs. 1,12,000 for your admission at Lotus Hospital on 25 September 2026 for treatment of diabetic ketoacidosis. Type 2 diabetes was declared by you as a pre-existing disease in the proposal form. Your policy with us commenced on 01 March 2025.

As per Clause 4.1 (Pre-existing Diseases) and Excl01 of your policy wording, expenses related to a pre-existing disease and its direct complications are excluded until the expiry of 48 months of continuous coverage after the inception of the first policy with the insurer. You have completed only 19 months of coverage with us.

Your claim therefore stands rejected.

You may write to our Grievance Redressal Officer if you wish to contest this decision.
