Subject: Rejection of Hospitalisation Claim

Dear Policyholder,

We refer to your claim for Rs. 1,18,000 for your admission at Citycare Hospital on 22 October 2026 for treatment of a fracture of the right femur. Your first health insurance policy with us commenced on 05 October 2026, and you have no prior health insurance policy with any insurer.

Our medical review of the case papers found that the fracture resulted from osteoporosis and was not caused by any accident.

As per Clause 4.2 (First 30-day Waiting Period) and Excl02 of your policy wording, expenses related to the treatment of any illness within 30 days from the first policy commencement date are excluded. The admission took place 17 days after commencement.

Your claim therefore stands rejected.

You may write to our Grievance Redressal Officer if you wish to contest this decision.
